# Product brief

## Audience

People with a messy technical problem, a bounded proof-of-concept need, an internal workflow to automate, or a system that needs investigation before a large commitment. Secondary audiences include technical collaborators and reviewers who want to inspect evidence.

## Job to be done

In under a minute, a visitor should understand who Kenneth is, what kinds of problems he can help with, see credible work, and choose whether to inspect a case study or describe a problem.

## Product promise

The site demonstrates versatility through a small set of well-evidenced projects, not a long technology inventory.

## Non-goals for V1

No CMS, login, dashboard, persistent backend, real-time system, blog engine, public telemetry, heavy 3D asset pipeline, fake metrics, or marketplace replacement.

## Success criteria

Visitors can identify the offer, reach evidence without the 3D hero, inspect limitations, use keyboard/touch/mobile paths, and contact Kenneth through a safe user-controlled channel. Measurement is deferred until a privacy and retention decision exists.
